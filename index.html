Case Study 01

Taskive

Connecting businesses with operational talent at scale

Overview

Taskive is a platform designed to connect businesses with operational talent across multiple roles, including virtual assistants, customer support, and product operations.

Problem

Hiring operational talent is fragmented. Businesses struggle to find reliable support, while skilled professionals lack structured visibility and fair access to opportunities.

Approach

Instead of building a simple listing platform, I focused on designing a system that supports different hiring models — direct hire, managed engagements, and one-time projects.

System Design

The platform was structured around three core portals: talent, client, and admin. Each portal was designed with distinct workflows, including vetting systems, contract generation, time tracking, and payment logic.

Talent Portal
Platform Core
Client Portal
Admin Portal

Execution

I led the product structure, defined workflows, and worked closely with engineering to implement a scalable system that supports multiple engagement types.

Impact

The result is a platform that not only connects talent to opportunities but also manages the operational complexity behind those engagements.

Reflection

"Marketplace products succeed when operational systems are as strong as user-facing features."

Case Study 02

Kemuko

Localized education that connects learning to opportunity

Overview

Kemuko is an e-learning platform built to deliver localized education, certifications, and professional development opportunities tailored to specific regional markets.

Problem

Many learning platforms are globally broad but locally disconnected. Learners need content that reflects their realities, career opportunities, and educational context.

Approach

I worked on structuring Kemuko as a scalable education platform with country-based relevance, flexible monetization thinking, and a stronger link between learning and economic opportunity.

System Design

The product model includes structured course categories, institution and instructor relationships, monetization options, and an AI tutor concept that can explain uploaded course material and respond to student questions through text and voice.

Course System
Learning Engine
AI Tutor

Execution

I shaped feature thinking, platform structure, product workflows, and future-facing AI learning experiences that improve content delivery and support student understanding.

Impact

Kemuko positions learning as more relevant, contextual, and career-aligned, while opening a path for scalable localized digital education.

Reflection

"Education products create stronger value when they are designed around context, not just content."

Case Study 03

Praxiom

AI-powered meeting intelligence and knowledge capture

Overview

Praxiom is an AI-powered meeting and knowledge assistant designed to help users capture, transcribe, summarize, and extract value from conversations and recordings.

Problem

Meetings generate a large amount of information, but much of that value is lost because notes are inconsistent, action points are scattered, and insights are difficult to retrieve later.

Approach

I approached the product as more than a transcription tool. The goal was to design an intelligent workflow that turns meeting content into structured knowledge and usable outcomes.

System Design

The product includes live and uploaded recording flows, transcription logic, summarization, action items, decision capture, future scheduling workflows, and shareable outputs. The design direction balances enterprise usability with a calm, premium interface.

Recording
AI Processing
Structured Output
Transcription → Summarization → Action Items → Decisions

Execution

I drove the product structure, experience definition, system logic, and overall narrative of how AI should support users before, during, and after meetings.

Impact

The result is a more thoughtful meeting assistant experience that helps users move from raw conversations to organized insight.

Reflection

"AI products are most useful when they are embedded into real workflows and reduce mental overhead rather than simply generating output."
